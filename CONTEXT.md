# Pickled

A chat-based meal planner for people in the Netherlands. It plans a week of meals around current supermarket deals, seasonal produce, and what it has learned about the person it plans for.

## Language

**Household**:
The people Pickled plans for, who share one Meal Plan and one Shopping List. Has a number of people to cook for. In v1 there is exactly one Household.
_Avoid_: User, account, family

**Meal Plan**:
A set of meals chosen for one week, built from current Deals, Seasonal Produce, and a Taste Profile. Its week runs from its Shopping Day (Saturday) to the following Friday, and it uses only the Deals valid on that Shopping Day.
_Avoid_: Menu, schedule, week plan

**Shopping Day**:
The day the Household does its one big shop for a Meal Plan, and the first day of that Meal Plan's week. In v1 it is always Saturday.
_Avoid_: Shop day, grocery day

**Deal**:
A time-limited discount on a product at Albert Heijn or Jumbo, valid for a specific date range and for a channel: in store, online, or both. The Household shops in store.
_Avoid_: Offer, promotion, bonus, aanbieding

**Seasonal Produce**:
Fruit and vegetables (including mushrooms) grown in the Netherlands that are in season in a given month. Each item is either fresh (being harvested that month) or stored (harvested earlier and sold from storage, such as apples or onions in winter). A Meal Plan uses the month that most of its days fall in. Produce available all year, such as potatoes or heated-greenhouse tomatoes, is not Seasonal Produce.
_Avoid_: Season food, local produce

**Recipe**:
Instructions and ingredients for one dish. In v1, Recipes are generated by the agent and saved for reuse.
_Avoid_: Dish, meal (a Meal is a slot in a Meal Plan that a Recipe fills)

**Conversation**:
The chat between the Household and the agent about one Meal Plan. Each Meal Plan has its own Conversation; anything worth keeping beyond that week becomes part of the Taste Profile.
_Avoid_: Chat, thread, session

**Shopping List**:
The ingredients needed for a Meal Plan, grouped so they can be bought, with Deals marked. One per Meal Plan, bought on its Shopping Day, usually all at one store; it splits across stores only when a Deal at the other store is clearly worth the trip.
_Avoid_: Grocery list, basket

**Taste Profile**:
What Pickled has learned about what someone likes and dislikes, built up over time from conversations and feedback.
_Avoid_: Preferences, memory, user profile

**Food Goal**:
An optional target someone sets for their eating, such as a protein minimum, a calorie range, a number of vegetarian days, or a weekly budget.
_Avoid_: Diet, nutrition target, constraint

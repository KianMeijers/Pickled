# Pickled

A chat-based meal planner for people in the Netherlands. It plans a week of meals around current supermarket deals, seasonal produce, and what it has learned about the person it plans for.

## Language

**Household**:
The people Pickled plans for, who share one Meal Plan and one Shopping List. Has a number of people to cook for. In v1 there is exactly one Household.
_Avoid_: User, account, family

**Meal Plan**:
A set of meals chosen for one week, built from current Deals, Seasonal Produce, and a Taste Profile. Its week runs from its Shopping Day (Saturday) to the following Friday, and it uses only the Deals valid on that Shopping Day. One Meal Plan per week; it starts as a draft and the Household accepts it.
_Avoid_: Menu, schedule, week plan

**Meal**:
The dinner on one day of a Meal Plan, filled by a Recipe for a number of servings, or by the leftovers of an earlier Meal. A day may have no Meal. Its meal cost is the price of what its Recipe actually uses, including a share of pantry Ingredients.
_Avoid_: Dish, dinner slot

**Shopping Day**:
The day the Household does its one big shop for a Meal Plan, and the first day of that Meal Plan's week. In v1 it is always Saturday.
_Avoid_: Shop day, grocery day

**Deal**:
A time-limited discount on a product, or a group of products, at Albert Heijn or Jumbo, valid for a specific date range and for a channel: in store, online, or both. The Household shops in store. Each Deal has a kind of discount (a percentage off, a fixed price, N for a price, buy N get M free, the Nth at half price, or something else), and where the store says so, the product's price and unit size. A Deal the store stops listing during its date range is withdrawn.
_Avoid_: Offer, promotion, bonus, aanbieding

**Ingredient**:
A kind of food Pickled knows, such as "kipfilet" or "rode kool", known by one name plus aliases. Recipes, Deals and Seasonal Produce all point to Ingredients. A pantry Ingredient (oil, salt, spices) is assumed to be in stock and is only listed for the Household to check. Each Ingredient has a rough number of days it keeps after the Shopping Day, so perishable Meals go early in the week.
_Avoid_: Item, food, product

**Product**:
Something a store sells for an Ingredient, with a price and a pack size. Each Ingredient has at most one chosen Product per store, picked once and reused; the Household can ask for a different one.
_Avoid_: Article, SKU, item

**Seasonal Produce**:
Fruit and vegetables (including mushrooms) grown in the Netherlands that are in season in a given month. Each item is either fresh (being harvested that month) or stored (harvested earlier and sold from storage, such as apples or onions in winter). A Meal Plan uses the month that most of its days fall in. Produce available all year, such as potatoes or heated-greenhouse tomatoes, is not Seasonal Produce.
_Avoid_: Season food, local produce

**Recipe**:
Instructions and ingredients for one dish. In v1, Recipes are generated by the agent and saved for reuse. A saved Recipe never changes; a variation on it is a new Recipe.
_Avoid_: Dish, meal (a Meal is a slot in a Meal Plan that a Recipe fills)

**Conversation**:
The chat between the Household and the agent about one Meal Plan. Each Meal Plan has its own Conversation; anything worth keeping beyond that week becomes part of the Taste Profile.
_Avoid_: Chat, thread, session

**Shopping List**:
The ingredients needed for a Meal Plan, grouped so they can be bought, with Deals marked. One per Meal Plan, bought on its Shopping Day, usually all at one store; it splits across stores only when a Deal at the other store is clearly worth the trip. The Household can add items of its own and tick items off. Its shopping cost is whole packs at their price, leaving out pantry Ingredients: what the Household pays at the till, and what a budget Food Goal is checked against.
_Avoid_: Grocery list, basket

**Taste Profile**:
What Pickled has learned about what someone likes and dislikes, built up over time from conversations and feedback. Kept as a few short notes that the agent writes and the Household can read and edit. The agent adds a note when the Household states a lasting preference, and reflects on each week's Conversation after the week ends; it never rewrites a note the Household wrote.
_Avoid_: Preferences, memory, user profile

**Food Goal**:
An optional target someone sets for their eating, such as a protein minimum, a calorie range, a number of vegetarian days, or a weekly budget. Nutrition goals are per person per day; a budget is for the whole Household per week. Because a Meal Plan only covers dinners, a nutrition goal is checked against the dinner share of the day (40%).
_Avoid_: Diet, nutrition target, constraint
